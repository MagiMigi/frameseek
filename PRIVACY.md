# FrameSeek Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

FrameSeek is a browser extension that adds a seek bar, skip shortcuts and playback speed controls to videos on instagram.com.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** FrameSeek does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

- No analytics, tracking, telemetry or advertising.
- No network requests of any kind. The extension contains no code that talks to a server.
- It does not read your messages, posts, account details or browsing history.

## What is stored on your device

FrameSeek saves only its own settings, using your browser's extension storage:
- whether it is on
- whether keyboard shortcuts, the speed controls and looping are on
- your skip length
- your last playback speed

If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser may sync these settings between your own devices. That is a browser feature; the settings are never sent to us. Removing the extension deletes them.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves the settings listed above.
- **Access to www.instagram.com**: needed to draw the seek bar on top of Instagram videos. The extension runs on no other site.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
